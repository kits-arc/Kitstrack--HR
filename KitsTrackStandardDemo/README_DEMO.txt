KitsTrack DEMO  (KAVIRI Information Technology Services)

A working copy of KitsTrack - Attendance & Payroll - with DUMMY data, for showing the product. It needs no licence key and
holds no real company's data. It stops working on the date shown in Settings > Licence.

START
1. Copy this whole folder to any normal folder (for example D:\KitsTrackStandardDemo) - not into Program Files.
2. Double-click KitsTrackStandardDemo.exe.
3. Sign in:   Admin  demo_admin / DemoAdmin#2026     Manager  demo_gm / DemoGM#2026     Root  demo_root / DemoRoot#2026

WHAT TO SHOW (about 10 minutes)
- Admin:   Dashboard (attendance and payroll insights) > Attendance & Punch Import (the day's punches, "Simulated punch" to
           make a live scan) > Exception & OT Approvals > Manual Permissions > Leave & Weekly-Off > Payroll Preparation.
- Manager: Payroll Approval (approve / return a run, DRAFT and FINAL Excel exports), Reports & Exports (daily attendance
           report, permission register), Settings (policy, appearance), Audit History.
- Root (demo_root): Organization Setup - choose Basic or Standard and tick Basic's features; the level is shown under Settings > Configuration.
- Version 0.6.0 features, all with dummy data: Departments & Designations, Shift Master & Planning (staffing plan shows short / over shifts),
  Leave Entitlements (balances), Fines & Deductions (Admin requests, Manager approves; some are waiting), Payslips (PDF) and Statutory Exports
  (PF / ESI / professional tax / bank file for the approved month-so-far run, muster roll, overtime register), Agents & Contractors, Shift Notifications.
- Three months of attendance and payroll are already in: one locked, one corrected and locked, one waiting for approval.
- Exception & OT Approvals: click rows to select them (amber for exceptions, green for overtime), or press Select all, then Approve / Reject
  them in one go. Remarks are optional.
- Reports & Exports: "Approvals & permissions tracker" lists every manual exception / overtime decision and permission (who, when,
  remarks) with Download Excel; "Attendance by date range" gives the attendance for any period.

Changes made in the demo stay in app_data inside this folder. To start again from the original dummy data, close the demo
and replace the app_data folder with a fresh copy from the zip.
